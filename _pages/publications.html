Journal Papers
  1. Partial Identification with Proxy of Latent Confoundings via Sum-of-ratios Fractional Programming: Extended
    Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    Informs Journal on Optimization (IJOO), 2026. TL;DR: This journal version extends the UAI 2024 PI-SFP framework for bounding causal effects from imperfect proxies via sum-of-ratios fractional programming.
  2. Adjusting Auxiliary Variables Under Approximate Neighborhood Interference
    Xin Lu, Yuhao Wang, Zhiheng Zhang (α-β order).
    Journal of the American Statistical Association (JASA), 2026. TL;DR: This work improves precision in randomized network experiments through auxiliary-variable adjustment under approximate neighborhood interference, complementing online-design methods with inference that does not require a correctly specified outcome model.
  3. A Minimax Learning Approach for Causal Inference Under Unmeasured Confounding with Negative Controls
    Nathan Kallus, Xiaojie Mao, Masatoshi Uehara, Zhiheng Zhang (α-β order).
    Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR), 2026 (Reject with encouragement to resubmit). TL;DR: This work studies minimax learning with negative controls for causal inference under unmeasured confounding, complementing the proxy-based partial-identification line.
Conference Papers
  1. Wasserstein Policy Learning for Distributional Outcomes
    Yiyan Huang, Cheuk Hang Leung, Qi Wu, Zhiheng Zhang (α-β order).
    COLT, 2026 (Top conference in learning theory). TL;DR: This work learns policies for distribution-valued outcomes with Wasserstein barycenters, connecting optimal-transport causal analysis to welfare optimization and giving regret guarantees governed by policy-class complexity.
  2. Causal Representation Learning with Optimal Compression and Complex Treatments
    Wanting Liang, Haoang Chi, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    ICML, 2026. TL;DR: This work derives principled balancing weights and treatment aggregation for scalable causal representation learning with complex treatments, complementing matrix completion through learned representations.
  3. Causal Matrix Completion under Multiple Treatments via Mixed Synthetic Nearest Neighbors
    Minrui Luo, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    ICML, 2026. TL;DR: Mixed Synthetic Nearest Neighbors shares information across treatment levels to improve causal matrix completion for data-scarce treatments while retaining finite-sample and asymptotic guarantees.
  4. Feasible Fusion: Constrained Joint Estimation under Structural Non-Overlap
    Yuxi Du, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎, Haoxuan Li, Cong Fang, Jixing Xu, Peng Zhen, Jiecheng Guo.
    ICML, 2026. TL;DR: This work constrains observational-data fitting with randomized-trial moment conditions under structural non-overlap, providing the estimation counterpart to budgeted experimental data collection.
  5. Budgeted Active Experimentation for Treatment Effect Estimation from Observational and Randomized Data
    Jiacan Gao, Xinyan Su (α-β order), Mingyuan Ma, Yiyan Huang, Xiao Xu, Xinrui Wan, Tianqi Gu, Enyun Yu, Jiecheng Guo, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    ICML, 2026. TL;DR: This work allocates a limited randomized-trial budget using observational priors plus uncertainty, overlap, and domain-discrepancy criteria, extending active effect estimation to settings with abundant biased logs.
  6. Partial Identification of Policy Values under Network Interference
    Ziyan Wang, Yiran Liu, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    ICML, 2026. TL;DR: This work bounds target-policy values when network interference and missing treatment overlap prevent point identification, connecting the interference and partial-identification strands of this research.
  7. Partial Identification under High-Dimensional Potential Outcomes and Confounders via Optimal Transport
    Yunfeng Wang, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎, Zijun Gao.
    ICML, 2026. TL;DR: This work combines low-dimensional signal transport with sliced-Wasserstein residual correction to obtain informative causal bounds in high dimensions, extending the partial-identification research line to high-dimensional potential outcomes and confounders.
  8. MLUBench: A Benchmark for Lifelong Unlearning Evaluation in MLLMs
    He Li, Haoang Chi, Qizhou Wang, Yunxin Mao, Zhiheng Zhang, Jie Tan, Tongliang Liu, Wenjing Yang, Bo Han.
    ICML, 2026. TL;DR: MLUBench benchmarks sequential unlearning in multimodal language models, identifies cumulative degradation and loss of cross-modal alignment, and introduces LUMoE to mitigate these failures.
  9. Treatment Responder Classification with Abstention
    Haoxiang Wang, Haoxuan Li, Ziyan Wang, Zhiheng Zhang, Aoqi Zuo, Erdun Gao, Kun Zhang, Mingming Gong.
    ICML, 2026 (Spotlight, <2%). TL;DR: This work connects treatment-responder classification with abstention to conditional value at risk, enabling doubly robust learning that defers uncertain cases and extends to violations of monotonicity and sensitivity to unmeasured confounding.
  10. SpaCellAgent: A Self-evolving LLM-Based Multi-Agent Framework for Trajectory Analysis
    Songhan Wang, Haoang Chi, He Li, Zhiheng Zhang, Jiayan Yuan, Cheems Wang, Hao Peng, Xinwang Liu, Wenjing Yang.
    KDD, 2026. TL;DR: SpaCellAgent automates spatial and single-cell trajectory analysis with LLM agents that plan workflows, select tools, and improve through feedback, extending this research program to computational biology.
  11. Online Experimental Design With Estimation–Regret Trade-off Under Network Interference
    Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎ ‡, Zichen Wang ‡.
    NeurIPS, 2025. Invited talk at POMSHK2026. TL;DR: This work uses exposure mappings to design online experiments under network interference with a provable Pareto trade-off between estimation accuracy and regret, linking causal inference to sequential decision-making.
  12. Design-Based Bandits Under Network Interference: Trade-Off Between Regret and Statistical Inference
    Zichen Wang ‡, Haoyang Hong ‡, Chuanhao Li, Haoxuan Li, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎, Huazheng Wang.
    NeurIPS, 2025. Invited talk at POMSHK2026. TL;DR: Extending the stochastic network-experiment framework to adversarial rewards, this work establishes the regret–estimation Pareto frontier and develops EXP3-N-CS with anytime-valid asymptotic confidence sequences.
  13. Unveiling Environmental Sensitivity of Individual Gains in Influence Maximization
    Xinyan Su, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎, Jiyan Qiu, Zhaojuan Yue, Jun Li.
    NeurIPS, 2025. TL;DR: CauIM brings causal effect modeling to influence maximization by selecting seeds through individual gains that vary with neighbors' activation states, with accelerated algorithms and performance and robustness guarantees.
  14. Active Treatment Effect Estimation via Limited Samples
    Zhiheng Zhang, Haoxiang Wang, Haoxuan Li, Zhouchen Lin.
    ICML, 2025. TL;DR: Complementing causal-identification work, this paper addresses efficient data acquisition through active treatment-effect estimation with finite-sample guarantees and sample-complexity analysis, including an extension to social networks.
  15. Tight Partial Identification of Causal Effects with Marginal Distribution of Unmeasured Confounders
    Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    ICML, 2024 (Spotlight, <2%). TL;DR: Using the marginal distribution of a discrete latent confounder without auxiliary proxies, this work derives tight closed-form causal bounds and an exact criterion for when that information improves identification.
  16. Partial Identification with Proxy of Latent Confoundings via Sum-of-ratios Fractional Programming
    Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎, Xinyan Su.
    UAI, 2024. TL;DR: Complementing bounds based on latent-confounder marginals, PI-SFP uses partial knowledge of the proxy–confounder relationship to compute valid causal bounds with global convergence guarantees, without requiring proxy invertibility or completeness.
  17. Robust Causal Inference for Recommender System to Overcome Noisy Confounders
    Zhiheng Zhang, Quanyu Dai, Xu Chen, Zhenhua Dong, Ruiming Tang.
    SIGIR, 2023. TL;DR: AT-IPS jointly trains propensity and recommendation models against worst-case confounder noise, establishing an accuracy–robustness trade-off that complements later work on causal inference with incomplete confounder information.
Preprints & Working Papers
  1. Rubrics on Trial: Evolving Rubrics from a Single Query via Synthetic Pairwise Evidence
    Haocheng Yang, Licheng Pan, Xiaoxi Li, Zhichao Chen, Zhiheng Zhang, Yuan Lu, Haoxuan Li, Hao Wang.
    Preprint, 2026. TL;DR: Rubrics on Trial evolves query-specific LLM evaluation criteria from synthetic response pairs, validating each proposed rubric without external annotations or model training.
  2. Denoised Conformal Alignment for Reliable Selection of Conditional Average Treatment Effect Predictions
    Xinyun Lu, Haoang Chi, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    Preprint, 2026. TL;DR: Moving from estimating treatment effects to selecting reliable predictions, Denoised Conformal Alignment subtracts an estimated conditional variance component from doubly robust error proxies and combines conformal calibration with false discovery rate control.
  3. Group Permutation Testing for Linear Models: Sharp Validity, Power Improvement, and Extension Beyond Exchangeability
    Zonghan Li ‡ ✉︎, Hongyi Zhou ‡, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    Preprint, 2026. TL;DR: This group-permutation framework establishes sharp finite-sample validity bounds for linear-model tests, improves power through design-aware permutation selection, and quantifies robustness when exchangeability fails.
  4. Orthogonal Uplift Learning with Permutation-Invariant Representations for Combinatorial Treatments
    Xinyan Su ‡, Jiacan Gao ‡, Mingyuan Ma, Xiao Xu, Xinrui Wan, Tianqi Gu, Enyun Yu, Jiecheng Guo, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    Preprint, 2026. TL;DR: This work represents combinatorial treatment policies as permutation-invariant mixtures and integrates them into an orthogonal uplift model, improving robustness to nuisance estimation and generalization across rare or perturbed policies.
  5. Individualized Causal Effects under Network Interference with Combinatorial Treatments
    Yunping Lu, Haoang Chi, Qirui Hu, Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    Preprint, 2026. TL;DR: Bringing network interference and combinatorial treatments into one individualized framework, this work combines rooted network configurations, doubly robust orthogonalization, and sparse spectral learning across exponentially large intervention spaces.
  6. Bounding the Largest Intersection Number of Regular Simplicial Partitions
    Zhiheng Zhang ✉︎.
    Working paper, 2025. TL;DR: Motivated by PI-SFP's branch-and-bound analysis, this note bounds how many simplices in a regular partition can share a point, connecting geometric regularity to the complexity of causal-bound optimization.
  7. Dynamic Window-level Granger Causality of Multi-channel Time Series
    Zhiheng Zhang, Wenbo Hu, Tian Tian, Jun Zhu.
    Working paper, 2025. TL;DR: DWGC uses sliding-window forecast tests and causality-based reweighting to detect changing Granger relationships between time-series channels, addressing temporal predictive dependence alongside intervention-effect research.

  1. Causal Inference under Real-world Constraints: Privacy, Robustness, and Pareto Balance
    Zhiheng Zhang, 2025. TL;DR: This thesis studies causal inference under privacy and robustness constraints, focusing on how Pareto principles balance competing objectives in real-world settings.
